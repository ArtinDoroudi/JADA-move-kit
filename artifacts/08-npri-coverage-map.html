Sources, method and limits
  • Who reports: ECCC NPRI bulk geolocation file, every facility that has ever reported, with its 6-digit NAICS code and year of last filed report. Facilities that last filed in an earlier year are counted as past reporters.
  • How many businesses exist: Statistics Canada table 33-10-1174-01, Canadian Business Counts with employees, June 2026. "10+ staff" adds the size classes from 10 employees up, as a rough stand-in for NPRI's 20,000 employee-hour threshold. It is an upper bound: most of these businesses never cross a substance threshold. Industries where NPRI reporting is incidental (offices, retail, trucking, government and similar) count as zero in this view so they don't swamp it.
  • Activities: JADA's working catalogue of 69 shared activities. The industry-to-activity mapping is our judgment from NAICS definitions and ECCC's sector guidance, not an ECCC dataset. "Typical" activities are the ones the industry's businesses commonly do; all of them must be supported before the industry turns green. "Occasional" ones are shown but not required. Mixed industries (for example 332810, coating and heat treating) list every specialty as typical, so they turn green only when the platform covers all of them. This mapping needs HSE review.
  • Status: stored with this page and shared by everyone who opens it. Anyone with edit access can change an activity's status from its panel.
  • Not modelled: facility-wide reporting duties every reporter has (off-site disposals and recycling transfers, facility details, filing), which the platform needs regardless of activity, and the oil and gas low-hours criteria.

Past reporters not on the map

These NAICS codes have reported before but have no 2025 reporters. They use an older NAICS code that now maps into a current one, or they were a one-off outside the usual NPRI industries.
