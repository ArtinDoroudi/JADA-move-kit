# NPRI guidelines and source material

- official/: supplied NPRI guide PDF and substance-list workbook; preserve the publication/reporting years in the source.
- extracted-text/: durable text extracts formerly stored under tmp/pdfs. These are useful for searching, but extraction can lose tables and formatting. Check the original source for interpretation.
- internal/: supplied VOC reporting steps; treat as internal/uncategorized guidance until authorship is verified.

The latter four extracts contain capture headers dated July 4, 2026 and source URLs. Capture dates are not publication dates. On 2026-11-04, `official/NPRI Guide (1).pdf` was verified as byte-identical to the supplied Downloads file `En81-1-2022-eng.pdf`: both are the 155-page 2022–2024 guide. The [2026 roadmap source register](../project-planning/welding-roadmap-2026/sources-and-decisions.md) records the hashes, supplied substance-workbook provenance, current online sources and unresolved discrepancies. The historical PDF is not the 2026 rule authority.

Research web captures are under ../research/source-captures/. Official sources, internal interpretations and client examples serve different purposes. This reorganization does not validate regulatory rules or establish which reporting year applies to a client.
