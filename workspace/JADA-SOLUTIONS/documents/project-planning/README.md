## How we will fill these in

Work through the baseline, scope, workflows, rules, and data in that order. Update earlier documents when later discussions reveal a dependency. Complete the overview after the details settle.

Use **Confirmed**, **Proposed**, or **Open** for decisions. A feature described in an older document is not verified implementation. A proposed requirement is not a delivery commitment. Record evidence and remaining questions rather than filling gaps with assumptions.

Milestone dates and effort commitments will follow the scope discussion.
