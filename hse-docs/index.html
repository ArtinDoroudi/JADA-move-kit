Welding calculator: documents for HSE review

JADA’s analysis of the ECCC Electric Arc Welding, Cutting and Spraying Calculator (2022-10-26 version), and how the NPRI app reproduces it. These are internal JADA working documents, not ECCC publications.

  1. Reverse engineering of the ECCC Excel calculator

    Every calculation path, input, default factor, coefficient and output in the 2022-10-26 welding, cutting and spraying workbook, with cell references and the workbook’s inconsistencies. Start here.

    Markdown: 01-workbook-calculation-guide.md
  2. How the app reproduces the workbook

    How the workbook becomes app data, and how inputs clients don’t know (grinding hours, electrode kg) are derived from purchases.

    Markdown: 02-workbook-reproduction-spec.md
  3. Proof: app = Excel (393/393)

    Results of running 393 scenarios through both the app and the official workbook. Every result matched.

    Markdown: 03-workbook-parity-results.md
  4. Defaults that need HSE approval (due 11 Dec 2026)

    The default values the app proposes when a client doesn’t know an input. An HSE practitioner needs to approve, change or reject each one.

    Markdown: 04-defaults-for-hse-approval.md
  5. Open questions for practitioners

    Interpretation questions where the Notice, the Guide and the workbook disagree or are silent, such as the stack NOx threshold and the Cr(VI) basis.

    Markdown: 05-decisions-risks-open-questions.md
  6. Substance list crosscheck (2025–2027 Notice)

    Check of the app’s NPRI substance and threshold list against the official workbook.

    Markdown: 06-regulation-crosscheck.md
  7. Technical appendix: first workbook recalculation test

    How the workbook was first recalculated outside Excel to prove it could be checked automatically. Technical.

    Markdown: 07-workbook-recalc-spike-findings.md
